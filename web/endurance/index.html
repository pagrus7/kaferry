What these runs establish

The application endurance profile uses one Kaferry process, key routing, high entropy payloads and separate single-broker source and destination clusters with replication factor 1. It measures the stated workload, rather than saturation capacity or replicated-cluster resilience. Rehearsals shorter than 24 hours do not establish 24 hours of continuous uptime.

Delivery time includes producer queueing, copying and verifier delay, from test submission to first unique receipt. Memory is sampled and can miss short peaks. Missing measurements remain unavailable; incomplete runs never establish successful reconciliation.
